# WakeForce — Promo Video Script (Hinglish, camera pe baat)

~55 second. Aap frame mein, beech beech mein phone ki screen recording cut-in.

---

## MAIN SCRIPT

**[0:00 — Aap camera pe, smile]**

"Hello bachcho, kaise ho sab?

Humari team ne ek alarm app banaya hai — aur main sach bata raha hoon, bahut hi shandaar bana hai."

**[0:07 — CUT-IN: app icon, alarm set hote hue]**

"Ab aap sochoge — sir, alarm app toh phone mein already hai. Usme naya kya hai?

Naya ye hai."

**[0:12 — CUT-IN: alarm bajti hui screen, swipe button nahi, JEE question saamne. Neend mein galat option → alarm aur tez → sahi answer → silence.]**

"Jab tak question solve nahi karoge, alarm band nahi hoga. Neend mein galat option daba diya? Aur tez ho jayega."

**[0:20 — Wapas aap camera pe]**

"Aankh khulte hi aapka pehla kaam kya hoga? Question solve karna.

Matlab subah uthte hi dimaag active state mein aa jayega. Woh jo aadha ghanta neend mein bistar pe pade rehte ho na — woh gaya."

**[0:30 — CUT-IN: mission complete screen, streak badhta hua]**

"Aur jab subah subah ek question sahi ho jaata hai na — woh feeling alag hi hoti hai. Poora din confidence rehta hai ki aaj ka khaata khul gaya."

**[0:38 — Wapas aap camera pe]**

"Toh ek baar use karke dekho. Aur agar accha lage, toh isko daily use karna — roz ka rakhna.

Ek hafte mein hi khud farak dikhega. Subah jaldi shuru hogi, toh poore din ki productivity apne aap badh jaayegi."

**[0:48 — Feedback ask, seedha camera mein]**

"Aur mujhe zaroor batana ki kaisa laga. Kya achha laga, kya pasand nahi aaya, aur isme kya improve kar sakte hain.

Ye app aap logon ke liye hi banaya hai.

Chalo — link neeche hai. Alarm laga do, kal subah milte hain."

**[0:58 — End card: app icon + QR code + "Download 👇" — 5 second hold, koi VO nahi]**

---

## "Daily use karo" wali line — 3 alternate

Jo aapko bolne mein natural lage, wo lo:

1. "Agar accha lage toh roz ka bana lo. Ek hafte baad khud farak dikhega — subah jaldi shuru hogi toh poora din khulta hai."
2. "Ek din use karke chhod mat dena. Roz karo. Discipline ek din mein nahi banta, streak se banta hai."
3. "Agar pasand aaye toh daily chalao — subah ka ek question, aur poore din ka mood set."

## Shooting notes

- **Sabse important shot**: alarm bajna → neend mein galat answer → alarm aur tez → sahi answer → silence. Asli phone ki screen recording, mockup nahi. Isko theek se record karo — baaki sab iske aage kam matter karta hai.
- **Script word-by-word mat padho.** Points yaad karke apne shabdon mein bolo. Bachche scripted tone turant pakad lete hain.
- **Streak number realistic rakho** — 25 ya 41 din, 365 nahi.
- **QR code 5 second hold** karo, taaki hostel wala bachcha phone uthake scan kar sake.
- **Koi rank guarantee nahi, koi fake testimonial nahi.**
